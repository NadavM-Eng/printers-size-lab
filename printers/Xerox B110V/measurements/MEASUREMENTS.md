# Xerox B110 (B110V_BI): measurements for the 3D model

Exact model: **Xerox B110 Printer**, configuration code **B110V_BI** (EU, and the code on the Israeli
listings), B110V/BIUK in the UK. Israeli and Polish shops write it as "Xerox B110V". The "V" and
"_BI" are Xerox configuration codes (BI = basic + wireless). It is a print-only, 20 ppm A4 mono laser
with USB and Wi-Fi and no Ethernet, launched in 2026 together with the B105 and B115 multifunction
printers. It replaces the Phaser 3020 and has the same compact Samsung-designed body as the HP Laser 107
series. Xerox gives the same 331 x 215 x 188 mm for both the B110 and the Phaser 3020; HP gives
331 x 215 x **178** mm for its twin, so the real height may be about 1 cm lower than Xerox's figure.
The model follows Xerox's 188 mm.

Frame: cm, Z up, front = -Y, back = +Y, x = 0 at the centre of the width (+x = the printer's right as
seen from the front, the side with the control panel), y = 0 at the centre of the depth, z = 0 under the feet.

## Scale and overall shape

Official closed size 33.1 x 21.5 x 18.8 cm (user guide, machine specifications). There is no straight view, so the
proportions come from the elevated studio photo (09) plus the drawings and video frames.

* 09 (front, about 20 deg above): bottom front edge 672 px wide, top front edge 704 px, back of the top 606 px
  (perspective). At row 200 the body is 702 px for 33.1 cm = 21.2 px/cm.
* Front columns and the Tray 1 door (rows 200, 300, 440 averaged): left column 4.4 cm, door 24.1 cm, right
  column 4.6 cm. The door is the whole white front between the columns from the feet to the top cover
  (no horizontal seam: rows 157-461 at the centre are one even white).
* White body height at the front: about 15.4 cm (front face 304 px, rows 157-461); a dark 0.4 cm shadow line
  under the top cover's front lip (rows 149-156).
* Back (drawing 02, vertical lines are true length): white back face 247 px, top cover band 47 px, so the
  white body is about 15.7 cm and the top cover about 3.0 cm of the 18.8 cm height. The top is flat.
* Model: feet 0.3 cm; white body z 0.3 to 15.8; top cover z 15.7 to 18.8 with the top outer edge
  rounded (about 1 cm); top cover corners rounded about 2 cm in plan; white body vertical edges about 1.5 cm.
* The top cover overhangs the white body by about 0.2 cm at the front (shadow line).

## Top cover

* Rim: front about 3 cm deep; left rim about 4.0 cm, right rim about 4.8 cm wide (the control panel sits on it),
  measured on 09 at row 100 (675 px = 33.1 cm): left 84 px, right 96 px.
* Output tray recess: x -12.5 to +11.7; the floor slopes down from the front rim (z about 17.9) to the paper
  exit (z about 16.3) at y about 0, where the black exit rollers show (09 rows 60-125).
* Hood over the paper exit: across the full width of the recess (09: x 130-580 px), from y about +1.5 to +8.6,
  top at z about 18.6, its front face over the black exit slot.
* Output support: a flap lying in the front centre of the tray floor, about 13 cm wide and 11 cm long, with a
  6 cm finger tab at the front rim (09: tab at x 290-420 px = -3.2 to +2.9 cm). It flips forward and up
  (13, 10, drawing 08) to about 40 deg above horizontal.
* Control panel: a raised dark pill on the right rim, about 2.4 x 7.5 cm, centre x +14.2, y -2;
  WPS button behind the power button, toner LED at the back end, status LED by the power button;
  "B110" printed in front of it (09, 15).
* Top cover itself opens for the cartridge (hinged at the back, about 75 deg; 07, 14). Not a site state.

## Front

* Logo badge on the Tray 1 door: dark grey rounded rectangle 4.8 x 1.4 cm with white "xerox", centre x -0.1,
  z 11.7 (09: badge x 305-407 px, rows 225-252).
* Tray 1 door: x -12.15 to +11.95, z 0.4 to 15.8, 1 cm thick. It hinges near its bottom edge, about 1 cm
  behind the front face (pivot y -9.6, z 2.2), and opens 90 deg to lie flat; it then reaches about 13.6 cm
  in front of the body, which matches the 350 mm "maximum depth" HP gives for the twin HP Laser 107w
  (Xerox gives no maximum size). With A4 loaded, the paper lies on the door.

## Back and cables (the important part)

* The back face is flat with a band of vent slots along the top (z about 12-14.6, x -8 to +10) and the rating
  label in the middle (x about -6.5 to +4, z 5 to 9). At the far corner (the left side seen from the front)
  a vertical rib stands about 0.2 cm proud (10, 02); the model puts the back face at y +10.55, the rib at +10.75.
* Port strip: a vertical strip about 4.5 cm wide at the back corner on the control-panel side (seen from
  behind: the LEFT end), with a screw at the top (z about 14), the USB-B port in the middle and the power inlet
  at the bottom (02, 10, 11).
  * USB-B: centre about 2.3 cm from the left edge seen from behind (x +14.25), z about 10.2
    (10: 10.0 cm; 02: 10.3 cm above the bottom).
  * Power inlet: a small upright rectangle, centre about 3.2 cm from the left edge seen from behind
    (x +13.35), z about 2.8 (10: 2.5 cm; 02: 2.8 cm). The official US cord in the unboxing video has a 2-prong
    plug, so the printer end is taken as an IEC C7 "figure-8" plug, mounted upright.
* Recess: the strip meets the back face along a thin seam; drawing 02 shows a double line there and the photo
  a hairline, so the strip sits only about 0.5 cm in. In 11 the plug's nose goes in and its whole body stays
  outside the outline. **Both sockets count as a flat back** under the user's rule: the plugs add to the depth.
* Nothing else sticks out at the back (no rear tray; the paper path is front-in, top-out).

## Correction: Tray 1 is only the LOWER front panel

The earlier notes above say the whole front between the columns folds down. That is wrong. Xerox's own video
frame 13 (B110 printing, badge still standing on the front) and the user-guide drawing 05 show that only the
lower part folds down; the upper front panel with the "xerox" badge is fixed. The Phaser 3020 photo 16 shows
the same. Measured on 16 (white body ~255 px for ~15.5 cm): the opening top is about 7 cm above the feet.

* Tray door: x -12.05 to +11.85, z 0.45 to 7.3, hinge at y -9.6, z 2.2, opens 90 deg.
* The open tray (door + fold-out extension, part `tray_ext`) reaches 13.5 cm in front of the body, so the
  depth with the tray open is 35.0 cm (HP gives 350 mm maximum depth for the twin HP Laser 107). The A4 stack
  lies on it and does not stick out past the tip in frame 13.
* Output support: flips forward to about 30 deg above horizontal (frames 10, 11, 13; 3020 photo 18), not 45.

## Phaser 3020 vs B110: same body

Compared the Phaser 3020 photos (16 Xerox hero image, 17-18 and the other OLX photos in
`work/photo_candidates/xerox-b110/`) with the B110 photos 01, 09, 10, 13, 15: same top cover with the
recessed output tray and hood, same pill-shaped two-button panel on the right rim, same side columns and
front panel, same right-side vent groups, same back vent band and label position, same output support with
slots. Differences: the panel text ("Phaser 3020" vs "B110") and the badge (3020: blue badge at the right
of the front panel; B110: grey badge in the centre). The bodies match; 3020 photos are used only for shape.

* Photos added: 16 (Phaser 3020 Xerox hero, tray open), 17 (Phaser 3020 back, vents + label),
  18 (Phaser 3020, output support out). The Haiku search found no real back photo of the B110 or of any
  sister; the back stays based on the B110 video frames 10-11 and drawing 02.
* Rounds: 3 (work/compare/xerox-b110/round0-2.png). Fixed: tray door is the lower panel only plus a
  fold-out extension; output support angle 30 deg; the upper front panel rendered black and a dark line at
  the bottom of the front (coplanar faces).
* Size: closed 33.1 x 21.5 x 18.9 cm (official 33.1 x 21.5 x 18.8). Printing 33.1 x 35.0 x 23.2 cm (tray
  open + support up). Refill 33.1 x 35.0 x 18.9 cm.
* Cables: power (C7, upright) and USB-B on a strip set in ~0.5 cm, both count as flat; cables reach 4.8 cm
  behind the back. specs.json `cable_space.behind_body_cm` = 4.8.
* Still differs / without a photo: the strip's exact recess (0.3-1 cm, taken 0.5), the plug lengths
  (typical values), the extension's exact length (from HP's 350 mm), the underside and feet (no photo),
  the left side (plain), and the cover colour renders brighter than the photos under the render lights
  (material is the measured #273d6f).
