# Pantum BP2300NW - measurements for the 3D model

Official closed size (Pantum EU product page and the BP2300 Series User Guide EN V1.4, p.31):
**W 337 x D 220 x H 179 mm**, 4.8 kg with the starter cartridge. Pantum gives no "maximum" (trays open) size.
The official depth is the closed body: front feeder door folded up, output tray folded into the top. Nothing
sticks out at the back except the plugs (the back corner pillars are part of the 220 mm).

Frame used below (same as the builder): cm, x = 0 at the centre of the width (+x = right seen from the front),
y = 0 at the centre of the depth (front face y = -11.0, back face y = +11.0), z = 0 under the feet.

## Front (02_front_straight, 15.25 px/cm; outline 514 x 271 px = 33.7 x 17.8 cm)

- Lid front face: top 1.2 cm (z 16.7-17.9). Plan corners of the lid r = 2.0 cm (05_top).
- Black trim band: 0.85-0.95 cm tall (z 15.8-16.7), set in ~0.25 cm (it shows 0.33 cm narrower each side).
- Finger grip in the band at the centre: 5.7 cm wide (darker), used to pull the feeder door.
- Front door (= feeder tray): x -14.15 ... +14.15 (28.3 cm wide; seams 2.6 cm from the left edge and 2.75 cm
  from the right), z 0.45-15.75. Flush with the pillars, thin seam lines.
- Side pillars 2.6-2.75 cm wide, the outer vertical edges rounded with r = 2.0 (same as the lid corners).
- Wi-Fi Certified sticker: 3.9-7.2 cm from the left edge, z 13.3-14.8.
- PANTUM logo: centred, 8.1 cm wide, ~1.0 cm tall, centre z 11.9, colour ~#3c3c3c.
- Feet/base: 0.35-0.4 cm dark gap at the bottom (feet near the corners).

## Side profile (19_sister_right_side, 31.4 px/cm; outline 697 x 558 px = 22.0 x 17.9 cm)

Box-like side, no slopes: the top is flat (the output tray flap lies in a shallow recess).
- Lid: 1.2 cm thick, spans y -10.95 ... +10.85 (21.8 cm), top edge rounded ~0.35 cm.
- Band: z 15.8-16.7 from the front (inset 0.2) to y = +10.4 (stops 0.6 cm before the back).
- Body side: y -11.0 ... +11.0 for z 1.4-14.8. Back-top groove: for z 14.8-16.7 the back is at y +10.4
  (lid hinge zone). Back-bottom notch at the corner pillars: below z ~1.2 the back is cut to y ~ +10.1
  (concave quarter, also visible in 03_34fl as a small dark notch).
- Front: vertical and flat, lid flush with the door.

Top surface height every 2 cm of depth: 17.9 cm everywhere (flat lid); the output tray flap sits 0.2 cm below
the lid top inside its recess.

## Top (05_top, 15.6 px/cm; outline 526 x 344 px = 33.7 x 22.0 cm)

- Output tray recess: x -11.5 ... +10.95 (22.4 cm, 0.25 cm left of centre), y -9.5 ... +6.7 (16.3 cm),
  i.e. 1.5 cm behind the front edge and 4.3 cm in front of the back edge.
- Output tray flap: y -9.45 ... +4.4 (14 cm) with a finger notch at the centre of its back edge; behind it a
  fixed strip (y +4.5 ... +6.7) over the paper exit (the exit slot with rollers appears when the flap is up).
- Control panel strip (pill): x +12.85 ... +15.25 (2.4 cm wide), y -9.8 ... +0.6 (10.4 cm).
  Buttons dia ~1.3 cm at x +14.05: power y -8.0, cancel y -5.5, Wi-Fi y -3.1 (centres).
- Model label (black, "BP2300NW"): x +12.85 ... +15.4, y +5.9 ... +6.8.
- Back corners of the body are almost square (r ~0.4); they show as small "ears" beyond the lid's rounded back
  corners in the top view.

## Back (14_manual_rear_view official drawing + 12_sister_back_ports photo)

Seen from behind, the ports are in the corner pillar on the LEFT, i.e. the printer's RIGHT side (x > 0).
- Back layout: two corner pillars ~4.5 cm wide at full depth (y +11.0); between them a rear panel with two vent
  grilles (upper half) and a label plate (lower centre), set ~0.4 cm in.
- Network (RJ45) and USB-B: one rounded pocket ~2.0 x 5.6 cm in the right pillar, centre x = +15.05 (1.8 cm from
  the right edge), RJ45 centre z = 12.0, USB-B centre z = 8.9 (3.1 cm apart). Pocket depth ~0.8 cm (the side
  wall of the pocket is clearly visible in the BP2300W close-up). The plug sits only partly in it.
- Power inlet (IEC C14, takes the C13 cord in the box), lower in the same pillar: centre x = +14.25
  (2.6 cm from the right edge), z = 2.0, mounted vertically in a shallow frame, recess ~0.5 cm.
- Nothing else sticks out at the back: no rear tray, no bulge.

### Cable space behind (user's rule)
Both sockets are on the flat back (only shallow pockets), so the plugs stick out: C13 plug ~3.4 cm + strain
relief 2.4 cm - 0.5 cm recess = ~5.3 cm, then the cable bends down; RJ45 2.4 + 1.6 - 0.8 = 3.2 cm.
**The C13 mains plug sets the space: about 6-6.5 cm from the back to the wall** (the "flat back" case, 5-8 cm).

## Moving parts

- Feeder tray = the whole front door: hinge along its bottom edge (y -11.0, z 0.45). It folds forward ~90 deg
  and lies flat on the shelf with its ribbed inner side up (15.3 cm long). A separate extension (in the box)
  with the light-blue paper stop continues it; with A4 the tray reaches ~21 cm in front of the body
  (07_input_tray_open: the tray looks as long as the printer is deep).
- Output tray flap: hinge along its front edge at the front of the recess (y -9.45, z 17.7); flips forward
  ~160 deg so it sticks out ~12 cm in front of the body, rising ~20 deg, with small stopper hooks at its end
  (11, 15, 16).
- Upper cover (toner access, not a site state): the whole top hinges up at the back to ~80 deg (17, 20).

## Colours (sRGB) and finish

- Body, door, pillars: #eeeeec, matte (photos 06/10/21 show a soft warm white).
- Lid top: #f3f3f1, satin.
- Trim band: #1c1c1e, glossy black. Grip notch #0f0f10.
- Logo: #3c3c3c. Wi-Fi sticker white with black text.
- Feeder tray inner side: #e6e6e3 with ribs. Paper stop and feed guides: light steel blue #8fb0dc (BP2300NW;
  the Malaysian BP2300W has grey guides).
- Exit slot / paper path: #1a1a1a. Rubber feet #2a2a2a.
- Buttons: white with thin grey rings (#b9b9b9) and grey icons; LEDs small (power green).
